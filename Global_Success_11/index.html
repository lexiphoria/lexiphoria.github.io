📝 Practice Tasks (Tasks 2, 3, 4)

Task 2 • True (T) or False (F)

Read the conversation again and decide whether the following statements are True (T) or False (F):

1. Nam has always had healthy habits.
💡 Explanation: False. Nam said: "I ate a lot of fast food, but now I prefer fresh fruits and vegetables." He used to eat burgers and chips and stay up late.
2. He has learnt the importance of exercise and healthy food.
💡 Explanation: True. Nam learnt from his grandfather that: "taking regular exercise and eating a balanced diet are the key to a long and healthy life."
3. Nam’s grandfather goes to sleep early, exercises everyday and eats healthily.
💡 Explanation: True. Nam shared: "Well, he does exercise every morning, goes to bed early, and eats a lot of vegetables."
Task 3 • Fill In The Blanks To Make Phrases

Fill in the blanks with the words: energy, habits, regular, balanced:

1. Full of : having strength and enthusiasm
2. Bad : things we often do that are not good for health
3. exercise: physical activity done frequently
4. diet: the correct types and amounts of food
Task 4 • Grammar: Past Simple vs Present Perfect

Complete the text based on the conversation in Task 1. Use the correct forms of the verbs in brackets:

In the past, Nam (1. eat) fast food and often stayed up late. But he (2. start) eating healthy food and (3. give) up bad habits. He has changed his lifestyle since he (4. visit) his grandfather, who (5. just celebrate) his 90th birthday.
