🧠 Grammar: Past Simple vs. Present Perfect
Understand when to use Past Simple (finished past events) versus Present Perfect (connected to present).
📘 Past Simple (S + V-ed / V2)
  • Usage: Describes events that started and finished at a specific time in the past.
  • Common Time Clues: yesterday, last night, 2 years ago, in 2020, when I was small.
  • Example: "You did a great job yesterday."
  • Example: "We lived in Ha Noi when I was little. Now we live in Hai Phong."
📗 Present Perfect (S + have/has + V3/ed)
  • Usage: Describes actions that started in the past and continue into the present, or recent actions with present results.
  • Common Time Clues: since, for, so far, recently, just, already, yet.
  • Example: "You have done a great job so far."
  • Example: "We have just moved to Hai Phong."
